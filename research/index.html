Research

My research interests span artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, and security. I'm particularly interested in how we build, train, and evaluate increasingly capable AI systems, how they behave in complex and adversarial environments, and how we can safely deploy them at scale.

// cat research_areas.md
[01]

Agentic AI & Autonomous Systems

Agent architectures, harness engineering, tool use, reasoning, planning, context management, and long-horizon autonomous task execution.

[02]

Model Post-Training & Reinforcement Learning

Supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, knowledge distillation, synthetic data generation, reward design, and the development of training environments for specialized model capabilities.

[03]

AI Capability Evaluation & Elicitation

Experimental methodologies, benchmarks, capability measurement, generalization, and the relationship between underlying model capabilities and the architectures through which they are elicited.

[04]

AI Alignment & Safety

Model behavior under distribution shift, reward hacking, emergent misalignment, dangerous capability evaluations, and the alignment of increasingly autonomous AI systems.

[05]

Multi-Agent Systems

Cooperation, competition, game theory, emergent behavior, and safety in environments where autonomous agents interact under shared or conflicting objectives.

[06]

AI Privacy & Security

The security and privacy properties of AI models and agentic systems, including adversarial manipulation, information leakage, training data privacy, and trust boundaries.

[07]

Autonomous Offensive Security

Applications of artificial intelligence to automated vulnerability discovery, vulnerability research, adversary simulation, attack surface analysis, and security testing across software, infrastructure, embedded systems, and cyber-physical environments.
